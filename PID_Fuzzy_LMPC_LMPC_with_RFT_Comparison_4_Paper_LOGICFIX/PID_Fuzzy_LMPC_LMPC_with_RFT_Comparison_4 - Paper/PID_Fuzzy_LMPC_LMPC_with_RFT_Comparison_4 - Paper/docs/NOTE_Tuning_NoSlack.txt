Why MPC may not increase endurance (no slack):
--------------------------------------------
If the mission demand profile (battery-side power demand) is fixed and must be met,
total required energy is essentially fixed. MPC cannot create energy; it can only:

  (i) reduce losses (mainly I^2R) by smoothing and limiting current peaks, and
 (ii) manage constraints more gracefully (voltage/temp margins).

Therefore, you should expect:
- clear improvements in stress metrics (Irms, Ipk, Vmin margin),
- modest endurance improvements at best,
- and endurance *worsening* if MPC tracks more aggressively than a baseline that
  occasionally under-delivers.

A CDC-relevant storyline (still no slack):
- Show MPC reduces Irms / peak current and increases voltage margin while keeping
  unmet energy below a small threshold (e.g., <= 1% of mission energy).
